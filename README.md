# Loading Screen Gold: A Tip in 200 Characters

Entry for make.fun's **u-ricoxyz-loading-screen-gold-a-tip-in-200-characters-2b1df270** bounty:
one sharp, funny loading-screen tip for stressed developers and AI coding agents,
200 characters or fewer.

`main.py` writes the tip to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` / `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the
grader). The tip is:

> Tip: your AI agent is staring at this screen too, waiting for you to approve the fix it wrote while you blinked.

112 characters, one line, no trailing newline.

Why this tip: the human stares at the loading screen, stressed — and the
turn is that the AI agent is staring at it too, waiting on the human to
approve the fix it already wrote while the human blinked. The joke is
that the human, not the machine, is the bottleneck this time. Both sides
of the keyboard feel seen, and it is not another coffee, deadline or
compile joke.
